How it works

From query history to a clearer next step

1. Compare growth

Compare a baseline month with a selected month. See actual query counts, active users, modelled cost, the percentage increase, and how the change splits across users, queries per user, and cost per query.

2. Find the drivers

Review repeated heavy queries, wide scans, peak-hour usage, costs by team and dashboard, and a forecast based on the available history.

3. Check the opportunity

Estimate possible savings, inspect the suggested query change, and measure before-and-after performance on the connected Exasol instance.

4. Review organisational spend

Teams can review budgets and trends, upload a file, enter a row, or capture a selected on-screen data area for local OCR review.

Know what the numbers mean. In this sample project, query history and departmental spend are synthetic demonstration data. Modelled cost uses (runtime seconds + scanned MB ÷ 100) × the selected rate card; it is an estimate, not an Exasol invoice. Live screen OCR runs in your browser and does not save anything until you review and submit rows.
